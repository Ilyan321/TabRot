# IdeaLab Hacks 2026 - Devpost Details

**Tagline:** A place for your next what-if. Build any idea into an app, game, tool, or creative prototype.  
**Theme:** Beginner Friendly | Design | Open Ended  
**Deadline:** Dec 1, 2026 @ 9:45am GMT+5 (Submissions close November 30, 2026 ET)  
**Format:** Online / Public / Students only  
**Prizes:** 4 non-cash recognition prizes (1st Place, 2nd Place, 3rd Place, 3 Runner-Ups)  

---

## The Prompt & Theme
> *"That tiny annoyance you keep noticing. The game you wish existed. The experiment you cannot stop thinking about. IdeaLab Hacks is a place to try it."*

- Choose your own problem, audience, and tools.
- Formats: App, website, game, useful automation, hardware prototype, creative experience, or cross-category experiment.
- AI is welcome, but never required.
- Small experiments, real possibilities: focused prototype that does one thing exceptionally well and tells a great story.

---

## Judging Criteria (Equal 25% weights)
1. **Creativity & Originality (25%):**
   - Fresh, thoughtful, or inventive idea.
   - Distinctive approach and team's own contribution.
2. **Execution & Functionality (25%):**
   - Prototype demonstrability and reliability.
   - Technical choices and scope accomplished.
   - *Note: Ties are broken first on Execution, then Creativity!*
3. **Usefulness & Experience (25%):**
   - Usability, design, UI/UX polish, accessibility, or creative impact for the intended audience.
4. **Clarity & Learning (25%):**
   - Clear presentation, demo video walkthrough, transparent tool credits, and honest lessons learned.

---

## Strategy / Winning Edge
- **Execution Tie-Breaker**: The prototype must **work cleanly** (zero brittle errors during demo).
- **Aesthetic / UX Advantage**: High-polish frontend or delightful micro-interactions stand out massively in beginner/open-ended hackathons.
- **Story & Learning**: A crisp "What If" question and an authentic learning narrative for Devpost.
