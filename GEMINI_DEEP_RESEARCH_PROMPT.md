# Master Prompt for Gemini Deep Research

Copy and paste the prompt below into **Gemini Advanced / Gemini with Deep Research enabled**:

```markdown
Act as a world-class hackathon strategist, product designer, and Principal Creative Technologist who has judged and won multiple top Devpost hackathons.

I am participating in "IdeaLab Hacks 2026", an open-ended, design-forward hackathon with the theme:
"Your next what-if. That tiny annoyance you keep noticing. The game you wish existed. The experiment you cannot stop thinking about."

### Hackathon Judging Criteria (Equal 25% weight each):
1. **Creativity & Originality (25%)**: How fresh, thoughtful, or inventive is the idea?
2. **Execution & Functionality (25%)**: How well the prototype works (Crucial: *ties are broken FIRST on Execution, then Creativity*). Must be a working, demo-able prototype that doesn't fail.
3. **Usefulness & Experience (25%)**: Exceptional UX, visual design, accessibility, and delight for the user.
4. **Clarity & Learning (25%)**: A clear, compelling "What-If" story and transparent demonstration of technical learning.

### Team / Execution Setup:
- We will generate core logic & complex code using Gemini Pro / Claude, and our local coding agent will assemble, wire, integrate, test, and debug the full stack into a rock-solid working prototype.
- We have 24 days, but we want a sharp, focused prototype that solves ONE core problem exceptionally well rather than a bloated half-baked app.
- Stack preference: Modern Web App (React / Next.js / Tailwind / modern canvas or web audio / lightweight APIs / local browser APIs / optional lightweight Gemini API).

### Your Research Objectives:
Conduct an in-depth deep research exploration to discover **5 standout, award-winning project ideas** tailored specifically to conquer this judging rubrics.

For each idea, provide:
1. **The Hook ("What If?" Question)**: A punchy, intriguing 1-liner question that hooks judges immediately on Devpost.
2. **The Problem / "Tiny Annoyance"**: What real, neglected human friction or creative gap does this address? (Avoid clichés like generic todo apps, basic study planners, or standard RAG PDF chat).
3. **The Core "Magic" Feature**: The single key interaction that proves the concept and drops jaws in a 2-minute video demo.
4. **Technical Architecture & "Secret Sauce"**: Exactly how it works under the hood, what APIs/libraries make it sing (e.g. Web Audio API, Web Speech, Canvas/WebGL, IndexedDB, CSS view transitions, or Gemini multimodal/reasoning), and why it is feasible to build reliably.
5. **Why It Dominates the Rubric**:
   - Why it scores 10/10 in **Originality** (unusual angle or fresh medium).
   - Why it secures **Execution** (zero brittle dependencies, deterministic and rock-solid to demo).
   - Why it excels in **UX/Delight** (visual appeal, micro-interactions, immediate visceral feedback).
   - The **Story/Learning Arc** for the Devpost write-up.

### Evaluation & Recommendation:
Compare all 5 ideas on a comparative score matrix (Originality, Build Feasibility, Wow-Factor, Demo-Readiness) and recommend the #1 overall winner that gives us the highest probability of winning 1st Place.
```
