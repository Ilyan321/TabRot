# 🎬 TabRot — Hackathon Video Demo Recording Guide & Script
**Target Duration:** 90 – 120 seconds (1.5 – 2 minutes max)  
**Hackathon:** IdeaLab Hacks 2026  
**Tools to Record:** OBS Studio, Loom, or Screen Recorder (1080p, 16:9 ratio)

---

## 🛠️ Step 0: Pre-Recording Checklist (2 Minutes)
1. **Have 6–10 realistic tabs open** in Google Chrome before recording:
   - A YouTube tutorial (paused).
   - A GitHub repo or PR.
   - A StackOverflow question.
   - An Amazon or shopping cart.
   - An academic paper or Wikipedia article.
   - The TabRot dashboard (`http://localhost:5173` or `https://tabrot.aenox.me` or extension dashboard).
2. **Audio Setup:**
   - Unmute your TabRot tab and ensure desktop audio is enabled in your recorder so the **water droplets** and **cremation crackles** are audible.
3. **Screen Resolution:** Record full screen at 1920x1080 for crisp text.

---

## ⏱️ Video Walkthrough & Voiceover Script

### 📍 [0:00 – 0:20] The Problem (The Hook)
- **What to show on screen:** Show your Chrome browser with multiple tabs open, scrolling past them.
- **Voiceover (Read this):**
  > *"Like almost every knowledge worker and developer, I suffer from chronic tab hoarding. You open a 3-hour tutorial video, save an academic paper, or leave shopping carts open 'just in case' — and 3 days later, your browser is consuming gigabytes of system memory.
  > 
  > Standard tab managers treat tabs like sterile spreadsheets that you open once and forget. We built **TabRot** to transform browser clutter into a living biological terrarium and searchable graveyard."*

---

### 📍 [0:20 – 0:45] The Extension HUD Popup
- **What to show on screen:** Click the **TabRot leaf/rot icon** in the Chrome toolbar to open the compact 380px HUD popup.
- **Voiceover (Read this):**
  > *"Clicking the TabRot extension icon opens our compact HUD. Here we see our live Ecosystem Diagnostics: average ecosystem rot percentage and active RAM usage.
  > 
  > TabRot tracks tab idle time through four natural lifecycle stages: Fresh (green), Stale (ochre), Rotten (rust red), and Fossilized (stone slate). The Priority Triage instantly surfaces the most neglected tabs draining your computer's resources."*

---

### 📍 [0:45 – 1:15] The Full Terrarium Dashboard & Live Interactions
- **What to show on screen:** Click **"FULL EXPAND"** in the popup to open the full-screen terrarium dashboard.
- **Voiceover (Read this):**
  > *"Expanding into the full dashboard, we enter the full specimen terrarium. Every tab displays its calibrated RAM footprint, idle time, and a discrete ASCII decay meter.
  > 
  > If we still need a tab, we click **'Water (CPR)'** to revive it back to 0% Fresh. Clicking or visiting tabs in Chrome also waters them automatically.
  > 
  > We can also click **'AI Diagnose'** — powered by Groq's LLM API — to generate dry, satirical roasts of our specific tab hoarding habits."*
- **Action on screen:** Click **"AI DIAGNOSE"** so the italic roasts appear on the cards!

---

### 📍 [1:15 – 1:40] The Cremation & Cemetery Archive
- **What to show on screen:** 
  1. Click **"CREMATE"** on a rotten tab (hear the procedural crackle sound!). The actual tab physically closes in Chrome.
  2. Click the **"CEMETERY ARCHIVE"** tab at the top.
- **Voiceover (Read this):**
  > *"When you're finally ready to let go of a tab, click **'Cremate'**. This physically closes the tab in Google Chrome and archives it with an epitaph, cause of death, and lifetime stats into our searchable Cemetery Archive — showing exactly how much RAM was freed.
  > 
  > And notice the audio: every click, water drop, and cremation crackle is synthesized live using native **Web Audio API oscillators** — zero MP3 or WAV files!"*

---

### 📍 [1:40 – 2:00] Closing & How to Try It
- **What to show on screen:** Briefly show the **Privacy & Disclosures** modal from the footer or the GitHub repository (`github.com/Ilyan321/TabRot`).
- **Voiceover (Read this):**
  > *"TabRot is 100% local-first with zero tracking cookies and complete Manifest V3 permission transparency.
  > 
  > It is open-source and can be installed in under 10 seconds with a single terminal command from our GitHub repository, or tested live on our web simulator at tabrot.aenox.me. 
  > 
  > Thank you for checking out TabRot!"*

## 💡 Quick Tips for the Presenter:
- Speak clearly and with energy — no need to rush.
- Pausing for 1 second between actions makes the video feel very polished and professional.
- Upload to **YouTube (Unlisted)** or **Loom**, and copy the link for Devpost!
